Anyone who knows me knows *I can't help tinkering* when there's room to *improve how I work*. These past few months *I've become the subject* of that myself: I've had my own workflow — from daily time tracking to architecture modeling — supported step by step by Anthropic's Claude.

This blog is where *I keep track* of that process. Not because the tooling itself is the story, but because I've noticed colleagues and friends in the field genuinely following along. As of today: *see my blog*. It also doubles as a record I'm building for myself — a kind of log of my own growth that I'll enjoy looking back through later.

**A bit about me.** I've been active in IT since 2002, after a [Master's in Computer Science](https://www.uantwerpen.be/nl/studeren/aanbod/alle-opleidingen/informatica-studeren/master/) with an initial [focus](https://www.researchgate.net/profile/Bart-Du-Bois) on [Software Evolution](https://link.springer.com/book/10.1007/978-3-540-76440-3) — with thanks to Prof. [Serge Demeyer](https://www.linkedin.com/in/sergedemeyer/), Prof. [Tom Mens](https://www.linkedin.com/in/tommens/), and Prof. [Jan Verelst](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jan_Verelst_(computer_scientist)). *Building and sharing knowledge* has stuck with me ever since: I taught at the University of Antwerp (through the [Lab on Reengineering](https://www.uantwerpen.be/en/research-groups/ansymo/laboratories/lore/)), and later built out the [XPLUS Academy](https://www.xplus.eu/xplus-academy/about/about-xplus-academy) — their training portfolio around architecture disciplines, architect assessments, and individual coaching tracks.

Since 2011 I've actively *modeled* with [ArchiMate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ArchiMate) (thanks to [Carl Seghers](https://www.linkedin.com/in/carlseghers/) at AXA Insurance), and since 2014 I've worked as an IT freelancer, initially through EntArch. Since 2024 I've combined the [SAFe](https://framework.scaledagile.com) roles of [System Architect](https://framework.scaledagile.com/system-architect) and [Product Owner](https://framework.scaledagile.com/product-owner) of the [System Team](https://framework.scaledagile.com/system-team) at my current client. For anyone familiar with the [DISC model](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/DISC_assessment): I'm a strongly blue profile — hence this pull toward structure, right down to how I build my own blog.

**How this blog comes together.** The blog itself is also an application of that same *way of working*: an Obsidian vault where ideas mature and get distilled first, with a deliberate anonymization and quality step, before they make their way to the actual site. In short: I apply to my own publication process what I describe in the series themselves.

<ZoomableImage src="/images/posts/tinkering-in-public/toolchain.svg" alt="Toolchain diagram showing Obsidian, GitHub, and Claude feeding relentless blogging, with GitHub Pages serving blog consultation to the reader" />

<details>
<summary>Reading notes</summary>

- At the top and bottom sits the business layer: "Relentless blogging" receives from both Vault management (Obsidian) and Editing (Claude) — both feed the same drive; "Blog consultation" receives from the deployed site, the reader's side.
- Obsidian and Claude each carry out their own function on what lives in GitHub: Obsidian manages the vault directly, Claude hosts the "Blog support" component with its two functions (Editing, Publishing).
- That Claude usage deliberately writes to the `bartmakehay.github.io` repo, not to the deployed site itself — GitHub itself carries the Deploy process that publishes to GitHub Pages.
- The handoff from Editing to Publishing is never automatic: it only happens after a deliberate anonymization confirmation.

</details>

Ultimately, I write this out of *a drive to make a difference* — and preferably one that outlasts my own involvement. This blog is an expression of that: sharing *my own progress in workflow support* with colleagues and a wider network, so that what I learn lands somewhere else too.

Follow along, and feel free to jump into whichever series interests you.
